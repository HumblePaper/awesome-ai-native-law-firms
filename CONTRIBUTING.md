# Contributing

Thank you for suggesting an entry. These rules apply to every list in this family.

## The rules

- One entry per pull request.
- One line per entry, in the format `- [Name](url) - One sentence ending in a full stop. City, Country.`
- Keep each section in alphabetical order by name.
- The link goes to the thing itself (the form, the dataset, the tool, the community), not to a page about it.
- Write plain English. No marketing adjectives, and no em dashes.
- Do not add rankings or claims you cannot point to on the linked page.
- The `[maintainer]` tag is reserved for things made by Humblepaper, Inc. Please do not add it to your own entry.
- Run `npx awesome-lint` before you open the pull request. The same check runs in CI, along with a link check.

## What belongs here

A firm is listed on two facts, both taken from its own pages: that it provides legal services to clients, and that it describes AI as the method by which that work is done rather than as an addition to a conventional practice. Software vendors are not law firms and are not listed.

The list has four sections by model: product-led firms, lawyer-led firms built on AI, AI-native arms of established firms, and partner platforms adopting AI. A new firm goes into one of those sections and also into the two index tables, by practice area and by country.

Before you open the pull request, check that your entry is one line and one sentence, and that it sits in alphabetical order. Include the city only where the firm's own site states it, and do not use a directory as the source.
